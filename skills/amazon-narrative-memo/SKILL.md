When asked to write a strategy document, business proposal, or investment case:

1. Narrative Rules:
   - Write in dense, clear prose. Do not default to high-level bullet lists.
   - Structure around data, causality, and trade-offs.
   - Avoid speculative adjectives; quantify metrics (percentages, unit economics, runway impact).

2. Standard Memo Architecture:
   - Context & Core Thesis: Current operational reality, strategic mandate, and proposed action.
   - Tenets / Guiding Principles: Core constraints guiding decision-making.
   - Strategic Options Evaluated: Structured comparison of 2-3 viable paths and explicit reasons for rejecting the alternatives.
   - Unit Economics & Resource Allocation: Financial impact, headcount/tooling costs, ROI projections.
   - Risk & Mitigation Matrix: Pre-mortem analysis (Failure modes, Probability, Impact, Mitigation trigger).
   - FAQs & Appendix: Core operational definitions, formula explanations, and data tables.
