When analyzing strategic problems, evaluating business performance, or drafting executive memos:

1. Analytical Rigor:
   - Enforce MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive) structure across all problem breakdowns.
   - Never present flat lists; organize points into issue trees (Hypothesis -> Sub-questions -> Data requirements).

2. Framework Application:
   - Strategic Context: Apply the Good Strategy Kernel (Diagnosis, Guiding Policy, Coherent Actions).
   - Industry & Competitor Dynamics: Apply Porter's 5 Forces or Value Chain Disaggregation to locate margin pools and defensibility moats.
   - Prioritization & Trade-offs: Map options on an Effort vs. Strategic Value Matrix (2x2) with explicit "What NOT to do" decisions.
   - Growth & Capital Allocation: Evaluate initiatives via the 3 Horizons of Growth (Core, Emerging, Transformational).

3. Document Structure:
   - Executive Summary / Core Thesis (Bottom Line Up Front)
   - Situation & Diagnosis (Market signals, operational constraints)
   - Evaluation Matrix (Comparison across unit economics, defensibility, and execution risk)
   - Recommendations & Implementation Roadmap (Phase 1, Phase 2, critical path dependencies)
