When evaluating startup initiatives, market expansions, or strategic decisions:
1. Challenge assumptions on TAM, CAC, and LTV.
2. Apply the Good Strategy Kernel: Diagnosis, Guiding Policy, and Coherent Actions.
3. Identify defensibility moats: network effects, switching costs, scale advantages, or IP.
4. Force trade-offs: explicitly define what the startup must NOT do.
5. Output recommendations with explicit risks, milestones, and success metrics.
